# Mnemora v1.15.0 — Graph Review Lifecycle Integrity

## Bounded review lifecycle

- Adds `kg_review` `kind: "worklist"`, a scope-local, cursor-paginated read
  model for pending self-link findings, rejected related-edge proposals, and
  invalidated pending proposals.
- The worklist is deliberately read-only. It rejects scan, preview, confirm,
  approval, repair, and decision arguments, and it never deletes graph facts,
  evidence, candidate history, or audit receipts.

## Durable stale-proposal safety

- A pending related-edge refinement or semantic proposal is durably marked
  `invalidated` when its exact `related_to` edge is retired, its cited evidence
  disappears or changes, or its endpoint evidence is no longer present in the
  same scope.
- Invalidated proposals cannot be previewed or confirmed. Rejected and
  accepted operator decisions remain historical records; they are not rewritten
  by lifecycle reconciliation.
- Adds schema v74 as an additive stale-review overlay. Migration does not scan,
  rewrite, retire, or delete graph data.

## Validation

- Adds regressions for stale fallback-edge invalidation, rejected-candidate and
  self-link worklist visibility, worklist non-mutation, and the v73→v74
  additive migration.
