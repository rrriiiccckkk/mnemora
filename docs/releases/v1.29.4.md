# Mnemora v1.29.4 — Safer Chinese lexical recall

## Chinese natural-language recall

- Segments continuous Chinese questions into bounded lexical candidates while
  retaining the original full phrase for exact matching.
- Lets manual recall find a source-linked Journal record such as “我喜欢喝乌龙茶”
  from a question such as “我喜欢喝什么茶”.

## Automatic-context precision

- Keeps automatic prompt attachment stricter than manual search: a lone Han
  character and the generic preference verb “喜欢” are not automatic anchors.
- Continues to attach a candidate when it contains a specific multi-character
  Chinese query anchor, such as “乌龙茶”.

## Verification

- Adds regressions for Chinese Journal recall and both the conservative and
  specific-anchor automatic-injection paths.
