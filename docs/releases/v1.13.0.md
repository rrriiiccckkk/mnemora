# Mnemora v1.13.0 — Related-Edge Legacy Refinement

## Measured topology decision

- v1.12's scope-local assessment remains diagnostic. Mnemora does not remove
  or uniformly downweight `related_to` in PPR or traversal: on the evaluated
  production graph, exclusion fragmented the structural projection and the
  measured downweight did not alter representative top-k results.
- `related_to` therefore remains a compatibility connectivity bridge while
  higher-information structural edges are introduced only through review.

## Review-only structural refinement

- `kg_review` now accepts `kind: "related_edge_refinements"`.
- An explicit bounded scan considers active, same-scope `related_to` edges
  only when their best retained observation has confidence at least `0.85` and
  its quote explicitly and directionally states `depends_on`, `part_of`, or
  `instance_of`.
- Scan and preview are read-only. An accepted preview/confirm operation copies
  the retained evidence to the replacement edge, retires exactly the reviewed
  legacy edge, and stores both an audit event and a durable receipt. Rejection
  preserves the legacy edge. No model calls, automatic relabelling, or global
  topology policy changes are introduced.

## Migration and compatibility

- Schema version advances from `71` to `72` with two additive local tables for
  refinement candidates and receipts. It does not scan, alter, relabel, or
  retire existing graph data during migration.
- Existing configuration and default PPR/traversal behavior are unchanged.

## Validation

- Added regression coverage for direct-evidence gating, scope isolation,
  preview-hash confirmation, exact-edge retirement, rejection immutability,
  hard-forget cleanup, and the v71-to-v72 additive migration.
