# Mnemora v1.2.1

## ReasoningMemory integrity hotfix

- Unified retrieval now excludes every admitted reasoning strategy whose
  per-memory delivery circuit is open. A harmful strategy therefore cannot
  bypass its circuit through the general memory-context path.
- Local semantic strategy retrieval applies the same circuit boundary before
  producing semantic hits.
- Delivery receipts remain immutable audit records after expiry, while current
  coverage and adoption metrics exclude expired receipts. Expired receipts also
  cannot accept later task-outcome feedback.
- Embedding backfill now rejects an embedder response whose provider/model does
  not match the explicitly requested reindex identity.
- Task-type classification uses normalized, token-aware English matching,
  preventing substring matches such as `api` inside `rapid`.

## Compatibility

No schema migration or configuration change. Existing delivery receipts remain
available for audit; the feedback summary adds `feedbackEligibleItems` and
`expiredItems` to make the active metric population explicit.
